## Keystone
- Primary action: cast Elementals from hand for Ashling's granted evoke {4} (or their own evoke) -> ETB, sacrifice.
- Capitalization: Ashling — "Whenever you sacrifice a nontoken Elemental, create a token that's a copy of it" -> second ETB plus a hasty body.
- Conversion to a win: hasty copies of fat Elementals (Avenger, Titan of Industry, Omnath Locus of Rage, Vernal Sovereign) + Jubilation/Return of the Wildspeaker pump; recursion (Muldrotha, Horde of Notions, Haunting Voyage, Greenwarden) re-buys the evoked cards.
- Nonbos found:
  - Bane of Progress — "destroy all artifacts and enchantments" hits the deck's own 5 rocks (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Chromatic Lantern, Timeless Lotus) and 7 enchantments (Abundant Growth, Fertile Ground, Cream of the Crop, Garruk's Uprising, Springleaf Parade, Hoofprints of the Stag, Descendants' Fury). Evoked through Ashling it happens *twice*.
  - Ashling's evoke only applies to spells "cast from your hand" — Muldrotha / Horde of Notions graveyard casts don't get it.
  - Destroy-based wipes (Shatter the Sky, Blasphemous Act) are not "sacrifice", so they never trigger Ashling.

## Commander role -> target shape
Ashling is an **amplifier** (doubles ETBs of whatever gets sacrificed) and a partial generator (grants evoke). Inverted triangle: broad generator base (35 Elementals/changelings — fine), few extra amplifiers, few payoffs.

## The core weakness: ways to sacrifice
Ashling's trigger needs a *sacrifice*. The only sources in the 99:
- Evoke (Ashling-granted, plus native: Foundation Breaker, Ingot Chewer, Mulldrifter, Shriekmaw, Slithermuse, Endurance, Fury) — always from hand, so every trigger costs a card and only happens on your main phase.
- Incandescent Soulstoke ("Sacrifice it at the beginning of the next end step").
- Descendants' Fury (only on combat damage).

Zero repeatable/instant-speed outlets. A hardcast Elemental that has already done its ETB can never be cashed in for a second ETB, and you can't respond to removal or your own wipe by sacrificing for a copy.

## Other weaknesses (lower priority)
- Mana base: ~12 lands that always enter tapped (Frontier Bivouac, Jungle Shrine, Sandsteppe Citadel, Opulent Palace, Seaside Citadel, Savage Lands, Raging Ravine, Boiling Rock Prison, Serpent's Pass, Misty Palms Oasis, Rain-Slicked Copse, Path of Ancestry) on a 4.25 average MV curve.
- Ramp is actually over-supplied (40 lands + ~15 ramp pieces), so it's tempo, not quantity, that's lacking.
- Filler: Hoofprints of the Stag, Cream of the Crop, Fertile Ground do little for the plan.
