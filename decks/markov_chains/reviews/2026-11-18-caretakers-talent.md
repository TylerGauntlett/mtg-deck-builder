## Verified text

`{2}{W}` Enchantment — Class.
- **L1:** "Whenever one or more tokens you control enter, draw a card. This ability
  triggers only once each turn."
- **L2 (`{W}`):** "When this Class becomes level 2, create a token that's a copy of
  target token you control." (Ruling: the copy doesn't copy counters or other
  non-copy effects.)
- **L3 (`{3}{W}`):** "Creature tokens you control get +2/+2."

## Best case

Edgar's eminence works from the command zone, so every Vampire spell (35 in the 99)
makes a token, even after a wipe. That makes L1 close to "draw a card every turn you
cast a Vampire" for no mana after the first 3. The draw helps both of the deck's
modes. L3 turns the 1/1 eminence tokens into 3/3s, and that includes tokens that
aren't Vampires (Bastion's Human Soldier, Idol's Eldrazi).

## Why no

1. **L1 is redundant with two cards that already turn the same event into the
   same output.** Compare them as effect × frequency × duration:
   - **Idol of Oblivion:** "{T}: Draw a card. Activate only if you created a token
     this turn." That's 1 card per turn, permanent, on the same condition, and
     it's a colourless 2-drop.
   - **Welcoming Vampire:** "Whenever one or more other creatures you control with
     power 2 or less enter, draw a card… once each turn." That's 1 card per turn,
     permanent, and it fires on the same tokens *plus* small nontoken creatures.
     It's also a 3-mana flier and a Vampire, so it makes its own eminence token.

   Talent would be the third copy of the same rate, so it is additive and not
   multiplicative. It also doesn't fix any gap. The deck's draw category is already
   about 13 deep (Skullclamp, Necropotence, Black Market Connections, Idol, Welcoming
   Vampire, High-Society Hunter, Baron, Gourmand, Recollector, Twilight Prophet,
   Henrika, Herald's Horn, War Room). The 10-09 review set a ceiling of two
   card-advantage adds. Recollector and Twilight Prophet used both, and
   Troublemakers was refused on 11-16 partly for that reason. Talent would be the
   third.
2. **L3 repeats the deck's lords at a worse rate.** The deck already runs Legion
   Lieutenant, Captivating Vampire, Stromkirk Captain, Edgar, Charmed Groom and Lord
   of Lineage (+2/+2). Almost every token it makes is a Vampire (Edgar, Bloodline
   Keeper, Coffin, Baron, Elenda, Charismatic Conqueror; Black Market Connections'
   changeling counts too). L3 adds a sixth anthem after **8 total mana** spread over
   several turns. The only bodies it pumps that the lords miss are Bastion's single
   Human Soldier and Idol's 10/10. Those cases are too rare to count.
3. **L2 is minor.** For `{W}` it copies one token, usually a 1/1.
4. **Marginal impact.** The games this deck loses aren't games where it ran out of
   cards. The deck has Necropotence. Talent is best when the board is already wide,
   and that's when the deck is already winning.

**If it were free:** the NO stands. Price ($12.31) is not part of the reasoning.

**Speed premise:** the open question from 11-02 (do games still run long?) doesn't
affect this verdict. The rejection rests on redundancy, not on the deck's clock.

**Community check:** Caretaker's Talent doesn't appear on Edgar's commander page or
on the Tokens or Vampires theme pages, so its inclusion is low. That agrees with the
NO for an independent reason: Edgar's token stream is already turned into cards by
Idol and Welcoming Vampire.

## No counter-proposal

The role it targets (turning tokens into cards) is already covered twice, so there's
no gap to fill.

## If you want it anyway

The like-for-like swap is **Idol of Oblivion**. It's the same trigger and rate, but
Idol costs 2 colourless while Talent costs 3 with a white pip (15 lands can pay W
for it; Voldaren Estate can't). You'd be trading Idol's late-game 10/10 sink for
Talent's L3 anthem. That's roughly even, so it isn't worth a swap.
