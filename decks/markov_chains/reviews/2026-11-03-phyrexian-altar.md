## Best case for the card

The deck has four sac outlets that cost no mana:
- **Viscera Seer**: "Sacrifice a creature"
- **Carrion Feeder**: "Sacrifice a creature"
- **Bartolomé del Presidio**: "Sacrifice another creature or artifact"
- **Phyrexian Tower**: "{T}, Sacrifice a creature: Add {B}{B}", once per turn because it taps

It also has **Master of Dark Rites**: "{T}, Sacrifice another creature: Add {B}{B}{B}", once per turn because it taps, and the mana can only be spent on Vampire, Cleric and Demon spells.

None of these can sacrifice without limit *and* make mana you can spend on anything. Phyrexian Altar would be the only one. It would turn Edgar's eminence tokens into mana for more Vampire spells, and each of those spells makes more tokens. The death payoffs (Blood Artist, Bastion of Remembrance, Grave Pact and others) fire either way.

## Tests

- **Combo veto (no early infinites):** passes. Commander Spellbook finds **0 new combos** with the Altar added. There are 16 near-combos, but every one needs a card the deck does not run (Gravecrawler, Forsaken Miner, Oathsworn Vampire, Cloudstone Curio, Nim Deathmantle and others). Adding the Altar alone does not add an infinite.
- **Role depth:** the deck already has four sac outlets that cost no mana. So the Altar's real job is mana, not sacrificing. That is a real job, but a narrower one than "sac outlet".
- **Community:** EDHREC shows 19.7% of Edgar Markov decks run it (10,363 of 52,525).
- **Budget (hard veto):** fails. $56.08 is more than $40.

## Would the answer change if the card were free?

**Yes, so the rejection depends on price alone.** If you already own a copy, or you decide to break the cap for this card, it becomes a real ADD candidate. The likely cut is one of the three free outlets that make no mana (Carrion Feeder first). The cut ranking has not been done yet. That needs its own pass.
