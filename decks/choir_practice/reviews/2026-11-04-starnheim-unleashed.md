# Starnheim Unleashed (2026-11-04): ADD IF

Moxfield list last updated 2026-11-03T17:02:50.213Z (sync exit 0, 100 cards).

## Verdict

**ADD IF your losses come from being out-rebuilt after board wipes. Otherwise NO.** If you do add it, **cut Tome of Legends**.

This is the same condition the 2026-10-02 mass-token review set for Entreat the Angels, and that condition has never been reported as met. Starnheim is a better card for that job than Entreat, so it **replaces Entreat** as the named candidate. Without that condition the deck doesn't need it. It already has 25 Angels and seven repeatable or mass Angel makers, and the pod's recorded way of beating you is ground attacks, which this card does nothing about.

## Verified text

> Starnheim Unleashed {2}{W}{W} — Sorcery
> Create a 4/4 white Angel Warrior creature token with flying and vigilance. If this spell was foretold, create X of those tokens instead.
> Foretell {X}{X}{W}

Rulings (2021-02-05):
- A foretold cast costs {2}{W} for one token, {4}{W} for two, {6}{W} for three.
- Foretelling is a special action you take on your own turn, and it can't be responded to.
- You can't cast a foretold sorcery until a later turn.

Colour identity W. Commander legal. Not a Game Changer. Commander Spellbook: 0 new combos, 0 near-combos.

## Best case

You foretell it on turn 2–4 for {2}. From then on it sits in exile, where wipes, discard and counterspells can't reach it until you cast it. After a wipe, including your own **Farewell** ("Exile all creatures"), you cast it for {6}{W}: three 4/4 flying, vigilant Angels. Each token is an Angel entering, so it triggers:

| Card | Clause | Per token |
|---|---|---|
| Giada, Font of Hope | "Each other Angel you control enters with an additional +1/+1 counter … for each Angel you already control" | Tokens entering together don't count each other (ruling cited in the 2026-10-02 review) |
| Bishop of Wings | "Whenever an Angel you control enters, you gain 4 life" | 4 life, which also triggers Thune, Lyra AoD and Exemplar |
| Righteous Valkyrie | "Whenever another Angel or Cleric you control enters, you gain life equal to that creature's toughness" | 4+ life |
| Cathars' Crusade | "Whenever a creature you control enters, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control" | a counter on everything |
| Seraph Sanctuary | "Whenever an Angel you control enters, you gain 1 life" | 1 life |

That's five verified synergy cards, which clears the three-card bar. **It isn't dead at either end of the curve.** You can hard-cast it on turn 4 for one 4/4 flier. As a late topdeck you can cast one Angel for 4, or foretell it and cast X=3–4 the next turn.

## Tests

**Not an Angel spell. This is the real cost.** It's a sorcery, so Giada's mana ("Spend this mana only to cast an Angel spell"), Starnheim Aspirant, Urza's Incubator, Herald's Horn, Folk Hero and Vanquisher's Banner do nothing for it. Pearl Medallion ("White spells you cast cost {1} less") reduces the cast, but not the {2} to foretell, because foretelling is a special action, not a spell. Three Tree City set to Angel can pay for it.

**Redundancy (effect × frequency × duration).** These cards in the list already make Angel tokens:

| Card | Effect | Frequency | Survives a creature wipe? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Luminarch Ascension | 4/4 flier for {1}{W} | repeatable, instant speed | yes |
| Court of Grace | 4/4 flier as monarch, otherwise a 1/1 Spirit | every upkeep | yes |
| Serra the Benevolent | −3: 4/4 flier with vigilance | once, then repeatable | yes |
| Ob Nixilis, the Ascended | 4/4 flier "each end step, if you gained life" | repeatable | no |
| Resplendent Angel | 4/4 at end step if 5+ life gained | repeatable | no |
| Valkyrie Harbinger | 4/4 at end step if 4+ life gained | repeatable | no |
| Emeria's Call | two 4/4 Angel Warriors for 7 | once | n/a (a spell, and an MDFC land in bad draws) |

Three engines that **survive a wipe** already exist. Starnheim's difference is that it's a burst of 3+ bodies at once, already banked before the wipe. The engines rebuild one 4/4 per turn. That difference only matters if rebuild speed after wipes decides your games.

**Marginal impact.** Does it change games you lose? The only recorded pod fact is that most wins come from ground attacks. The deck's answers to that are defensive (Ghostly Prison, Archangel of Tithes, Magus of the Moat, Authority of the Consuls, Herald of Eternal Dawn). Starnheim adds three 4/4 vigilant blockers, which helps a little, but only from turn 5+ and only after spending {2} earlier. Threat count isn't the deck's gap. **This is a wipe-recovery card**, and whether it earns a slot depends on how often you lose to wipes. I don't know that. You do.

**Worst-case draw.**
- Opening hand: foretell on turn 3 (after Giada on turn 2), then X=2 on turn 5 gives two Angels that grow with Giada's counters. That's fine.
- Turn-12 topdeck on an empty board: one 4/4 for 4 this turn, or three next turn. That's weaker than Emeria's Call's immediate two, but it isn't dead.
- **This card has no bad end of the curve. Its only weakness is redundancy.**

**Bracket fit.** No combos, not a Game Changer, no tutoring. Fine.

**Disagreement check.** EDHREC: **14.1% of Giada decks (5,517 / 39,240).** That's moderate, not a staple, and it lines up with my verdict. My independent reason: Giada decks that run it are mostly ones without this list's three wipe-proof Angel engines (Luminarch was added 2026-11-01).

**Price:** $1.81, fetched 2026-11-04 15:10 UTC. **Price plays no part in this verdict.** The condition and the cut would be the same if it were free.

## The cut, if the condition holds: Tome of Legends

- **Tome was already the agreed cut.** The 2026-10-30 review chose "ADD Battle Angels of Tyr, cut Tome of Legends", and the 2026-10-30 Authority review was written assuming "Tome of Legends out". **The Moxfield list still has Tome of Legends.** Either the swap was never made, or Tome went back in. **Confirm which.** If you put it back on purpose, tell me why, because that reverses this cut.
- **Caveat on draw:** Dawn of Hope, which was in the card-draw count the 2026-10-30 review used to justify cutting Tome, **is not in the current Moxfield list.** So draw is one source thinner than when Tome was ranked last. The remaining draw spells are Folk Hero, Vanquisher's Banner, Exemplar of Light, Inspiring Overseer, Wojek Investigator, Court of Grace (monarch) and Battle Angels of Tyr, plus Minas Tirith, War Room and Bonders' Enclave. That's still enough to cut Tome, but less comfortably.

Runners-up, spared:
1. **Emeria's Call.** It's the same role and Starnheim scales better, but Call is also a land, so cutting it costs a mana source in bad draws.
2. **Herald of the Host.** It's an Angel spell that every reducer discounts, and each myriad copy triggers Giada, Bishop, Valkyrie and Crusade every attack. It already does Starnheim's job every combat.
3. **Valorous Stance / Fateful Absence.** Still reserved for Teferi's Protection and Darksteel Mutation, per the 2026-11-01 review.

## What would change the verdict

- **To ADD:** you report losing games after a wipe because the pod redeploys faster than you, while Luminarch, Court of Grace and Serra are absent or too slow. You can also tell me you're winning with Farewell often. Foretell before Farewell, then three Angels the turn after, is the strongest line this card has.
- **To a firm NO:** you're losing to ground attacks before wipes ever happen, in which case defensive slots matter more than another threat.
