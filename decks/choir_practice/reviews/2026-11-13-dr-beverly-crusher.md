## The card (Scryfall, 2026-11-13)

> {2}{W} Legendary Creature — Human Doctor, 2/2
> If you gaining life causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time.
> Whenever you gain life, put a +1/+1 counter on Dr. Crusher.

Scope: it doubles only triggers *caused by* gaining life ("whenever you gain life") on **your** permanents. It does not touch end-step "if you gained life this turn" checks (they trigger at the start of the end step, not on gaining life), static thresholds, or replacement effects.

## What it doubles in this deck

Every "whenever you gain life" trigger on a permanent in the current 99:

| Card | Clause | Doubled? |
|---|---|---|
| Archangel of Thune | "Whenever you gain life, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control." | Yes |
| Lyra, Archangel of Dawn | "Whenever you gain life, put a +1/+1 counter on each Angel you control." | Yes |
| Exemplar of Light | "Whenever you gain life, put a +1/+1 counter on this creature." (draw is "only once each turn") | Yes, but only an extra counter. No extra card |
| Dr. Crusher itself | "Whenever you gain life, put a +1/+1 counter on Dr. Crusher." | Yes, +2 per event |

**Not doubled** (they look like lifegain payoffs but aren't triggered by gaining life): Resplendent Angel, Valkyrie Harbinger, Ob Nixilis (end-step checks), Righteous Valkyrie (+2/+2 is static), Angel of Destiny (wins at end step), Angel of Vitality (replacement), Bishop of Wings and Seraph Sanctuary (trigger on an Angel entering).

Dawn of Hope, which older reviews counted as a payoff, is no longer in the list.

That leaves **two real targets**: Thune and Lyra AoD.

## Steel-man

With Thune out, every lifegain event (Bishop of Wings, Righteous Valkyrie, Seraph Sanctuary, each lifelink damage step) puts two counters on every creature. The rubric favours multipliers over additive cards, and this is a multiplier on the deck's bridge from going wide to growing big.

## Why it fails

1. **It multiplies a two-card package.** Crusher only does its job with Thune or Lyra AoD on the battlefield. Drawing about 14 cards by turn 6, you see at least one of those two about 26% of the time, before either gets removed. In roughly three games of four, Crusher is a 3-mana ground 2/2 that grows by 2 per lifegain event.
2. **The doubling it offers is already in the deck.** Thune with Lyra AoD already puts two counters on each Angel per event. Crusher with Thune does the same thing. Crusher doesn't add a new effect. It's a third, weaker copy of a pairing the deck already has, and it's the only copy that does nothing by itself.
3. **It's a non-Angel in an Angel deck.** It gets no Giada counter or Giada mana. Starnheim Aspirant, Urza's Incubator and Herald's Horn don't discount it, Lyra Dawnbringer doesn't give it lifelink, Folk Hero doesn't draw off it, and its entry doesn't trigger Bishop of Wings, Righteous Valkyrie or Seraph Sanctuary. Every Angel at 3 mana in the list does more on arrival.
4. **Its own body works against the deck.** It has no flying, and Magus of the Moat ("Creatures without flying can't attack") stops it attacking. As a big ground blocker it adds to a role that Ghostly Prison, Archangel of Tithes and Magus already cover.

Synergy test: it names only two real partner cards (three if you count Exemplar's extra counter). That just meets the three-card bar and doesn't clear it convincingly.

The NO doesn't depend on price. It would still be a NO if the card were free.

## Community check

It doesn't appear on Giada's page, and it peaks around 11% even in dedicated lifegain commanders. Here the community and I agree for an independent reason: Giada decks get their doubling from Angels, and this card isn't one.

## Cut (for reference only)

No cut is named because the card doesn't earn a slot. Tome of Legends is still the weakest card and is already claimed by two conditional adds (Firemane Commando 11-08, Starnheim Unleashed 11-04).

## What would change this

At least two more "whenever you gain life" payoffs on permanents in the 99, so Crusher has something to double in most games instead of one game in four. No mono-white legal card found by search fills that today. The search returned Vanguard Seraph (counters itself only), Nykthos Paragon (6 mana, once a turn) and Valgavoth (not Commander-legal), and none of them is an add.
