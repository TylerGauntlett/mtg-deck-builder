## The card (Scryfall, 2026-11-13)

> {2}{U}{U} Legendary Creature — Vulcan Human Scientist, 2/4
> If you would draw a card, draw two cards instead.

It's a mandatory replacement effect with no "may", so every draw doubles, including the draw step.

## Steel-man

The deck turns lands into cards, and this doubles every one of those cards:
- Aesi ("Landfall — … you may draw a card") and Tatyova ("Landfall — … draw a card") draw 2 per land.
- With Ancient Greenwarden ("that ability triggers an additional time"), they draw 4 per land.
- Rhystic Study, Nezahal, Growth Spiral, Urban Evolution (6 cards), Cultivator Colossus and the draw step all double too.

At MV4 it also fills the deck's thinnest curve slot. MV4 has only 3 cards today: Arixmethes, Oracle of Mul Daya and Nissa, Worldsoul Speaker.

## Why it fails

1. **Card draw is already the deck's deepest category.** It runs four repeatable draw engines: Aesi, Tatyova, Rhystic Study and Nezahal. Incidental draw adds Arcane Denial, Growth Spiral, Urban Evolution, Cultivator Colossus, Yavimaya Elder, Herd Heirloom and Waterlogged Grove. Reliquary Tower, Thought Vessel and Nezahal exist because the hand overflows. The 2026-09-01 audit found the gaps were interaction, mass answers and graveyard hate, not cards. Spock is a multiplier, which the rubric favours, but it multiplies a resource the deck doesn't run short of. It adds no interaction.
2. **It speeds up decking, in a pod that runs mill.** The audit already flagged this: "nothing in the 99 handles *library* size", with a mill deck in the pod. Spock makes the mandatory draws double:
   - Tatyova (no "may")
   - Nezahal ("draw a card", on every opponent noncreature spell)
   - Rhystic Study, once you accept a draw
   - the draw step

   Aesi's draw is optional, so you can decline it. The rest you can't turn off unless Spock leaves. Tatyova and Greenwarden together with Spock draw 4 cards per land, with up to 5 land drops a turn. That combines with an opponent's mill in exactly the game this pod produces.
3. **It's a 2/4 for 4 that does nothing on entry.** It has no landfall or land text (the deck's tiebreak preference), and it's a removal magnet that only helps if it survives a turn cycle. Most of the cards in the list at MV3–4 put a land or a body onto the board the turn they come down.

The NO doesn't depend on price; it would still be a NO if the card were free.

## Community check

It doesn't appear on Aesi's page. It's played almost entirely in Star Trek-themed decks. I see no reason this deck is different: lands decks don't lack cards.

## Cut (for reference only)

If you add it anyway, the weakest cards in the current list are still the ones the 2026-09-01 audit named and you haven't swapped: **Jin-Gitaxias, Progress Tyrant** (MV7, no land text), then Herd Heirloom and Negate. They were marked for interaction upgrades, though. Spending one on more card draw undoes that plan.

## What would change this

- **You report games lost with an empty hand**: the engines are dying before they draw and you run out of gas. That's new evidence that draw is short, and I'd re-run this.
- **The pod no longer has a mill deck, and you add a library-refill card** (Gaea's Blessing, as the audit suggested). That removes objection 2. Objection 1 still stands.
